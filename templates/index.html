About

Who I Am & What I Do

I'm a Data Analyst & Business Intelligence Specialist based in Multan, Pakistan, passionate about transforming complex, messy data into clear, actionable insights that drive real business decisions.

I specialize in building end-to-end analytics systems — not just dashboards, but scalable solutions that help organizations truly understand their performance and make confident decisions.

Currently at Dream Edge Technologies, I analyze mobile app performance and monetization metrics across a portfolio of 10+ apps. Using Firebase Analytics and GA4, I track user funnels, retention cohorts, ARPU, LTV, and subscription behavior at scale.

Previously at Blue Cascade Pakistan, I designed and automated a lead generation system that delivered 300+ qualified B2B leads daily with zero manual effort — significantly improving sales efficiency.

My technical toolkit includes SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau, n8n, Selenium, and Machine Learning. Whether it's data modeling, automation, or predictive analytics, I focus on delivering measurable business impact.

Beyond professional work, I actively give back to the community as Data Science Lead at The Youth Matrix and as a Microsoft Learn Student Ambassador. I strongly believe that the real power of data lies in sharing knowledge and empowering others.
